Roadmap

Language packs are planned.

VeilType does not currently ship offline speech models. Optional language packs are planned for a future voice typing upgrade.

The goal is simple: if you only need English and Russian, you should not store Turkish, German, Spanish, or other models on your phone.

Language pack flow

  • Keep the current keyboard app lightweight.
  • Add an Offline Languages screen later.
  • Download only selected language packs.
  • Use voice typing offline after the future upgrade is installed.
  • Delete unused languages anytime.
