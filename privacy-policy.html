VeilType Privacy Policy

VeilType is an Android keyboard that encrypts text locally before you paste or send it through another app.

Data we do not collect

VeilType does not operate a message-content server, does not require an account, and does not upload typed text, encrypted messages, passwords, or shared secrets to a VeilType server.

Permissions

The app may request camera or microphone permissions only for optional input features such as camera media or voice input. Core text encryption works without sending content to a VeilType server.

Support

When you email support, we receive the information you choose to send in that email.
