Roadmap

Offline voice is planned next.

VeilType does not currently include offline dictation. It is planned as a future upgrade for private messages.

The intended design: download selected language packs, recognize speech locally, review the text, then encrypt before sending.

Honest limits

  • Recognition quality depends on the language model and microphone.
  • Background noise and slow devices can reduce accuracy.
  • No current claim that offline dictation is already available.
  • The planned design avoids a VeilType transcription server.
  • Users should be able to edit recognized text before encryption.
